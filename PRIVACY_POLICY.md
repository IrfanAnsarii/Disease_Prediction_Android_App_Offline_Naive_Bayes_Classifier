# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 2026  

## 1. Introduction

Disease Prediction is a fully offline Android application that predicts possible diseases based on user-selected symptoms using a locally implemented Naive Bayes classifier.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

---

## 2. No Data Collection

This application does **NOT collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal or sensitive user data.

---

## 3. Personal Information

The app does not collect personal information such as:

- Name  
- Email address  
- Phone number  
- Location data  
- Contacts  
- Device identifiers  

---

## 4. Health Information

Symptoms selected by users are processed locally on the device only for generating predictions.

- No health-related data is stored.
- No health-related data is transmitted.
- All processing happens entirely offline.

---

## 5. Internet Usage

This application does not require an internet connection and does not communicate with any external servers.

---

## 6. Data Storage

All calculations are performed in-memory on the user’s device.  
The app does not store or retain any user-entered data.

---

## 7. Third-Party Services

This app does not use:

- Third-party analytics tools  
- Advertising SDKs  
- Tracking technologies  
- External APIs  

---

## 8. Permissions

The application does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as:

- Location  
- Camera  
- Microphone  
- Contacts  
- Storage  

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

---

## 10. Security

Since no user data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of external data exposure from this application.

---

## 11. Medical Disclaimer

This application is intended for educational purposes only.

It is **NOT** a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.  
Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

---

## 12. Changes to This Policy

If the app functionality changes in the future to include data collection, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

---

## 13. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
